How it works

Paste a CSV exported from Tally or Zoho (or any spreadsheet). Choose a preset to auto-map columns, or map manually. Masters (accounts, parties, items) are created; documents (invoices, vouchers) are created and submitted. Ledger opening balances are carried into the books via an Opening Balance equity leg, so your Trial Balance reflects history on day one.
